# Repaired apostrophes, comment threads, and a calmer solo editor

First, an apology and a fix.

## If your story filled up with "amp;amp;amp;"

- Some solo stories showed every quote mark and apostrophe as a long string of "amp;amp;amp;" that grew each time the story saved. That was our bug, not anything you did.
- It is fixed. Saving no longer does it, and an affected story is put right the moment you open it. There is nothing to do on your side.
- We checked every story. No words were lost.
- Word counts are right again too: a word with an apostrophe counts as one word.

> If anything in your story still looks wrong, tell an admin and we will look at it with you.

## Comments are conversations now

- Anyone who can comment can reply, and every voice wears an "author" or "beta" tag.
- The author decides: Resolve or Reject on a note, Accept or Reject on a suggested rewrite. A reader can resolve their own note.
- Edit or delete your own words from the ⋮ menu. The author can remove a reader's note but never rewrite it.
- Closed threads fold to one line. Click one to read it again.
- A beta reader's comment no longer interrupts the author's typing or saving. There is more about that in the previous post.

## A calmer solo editor

- Your story saves itself every 30 seconds once you pause typing, without moving your caret.
- If the same story is open in two tabs, they can't overwrite each other. You are asked which copy to keep.
- Three menus instead of a crowded row: Share, Document and Appearance.
- Find and replace: Ctrl or ⌘ + F.
- Share / Download: copy the formatted story, or download it as a web page or a PDF.
- Chapter arrows sit at the foot of the editor.
- A chapter that is too long is refused with a message asking you to split it, never quietly trimmed.

## In a game

- Everyone hears the clock through the last ten seconds of a turn, spectators too.
- The Galaga screen says how to fire and steer.
- The SuperSoaker is a proper water pistol again.
