# Approval voting, chat reactions, and a new way around the dashboard

Here is what changed this week.

## Voting is fairer

- Back as many scenarios as you like on the vote card. Every click is a toggle, and the tally counts each one.
- Everyone marks Ready when they are done, and the host can start only once every connected writer is ready. Nothing starts on its own any more.
- Rerolling one scenario drops only its votes. A fresh deal clears the board.

## React to chat

- Add an emoji to any writer's or watcher's chat line. Pick from a searchable grid; the chips sit under the message.
- Hover a chip and it names everyone who reacted, at once, in their own colours.
- Reactions stay with the story, so they are there after a refresh.

## Any colour you like

- Settings now has a colour picker and a hex box beside the palette. Pick any colour and it saves on its own.

## Finding your way around

- The dashboard rail is grouped: Cowrite, Solo writes and Account each open a small menu beside the row. On a phone the menu folds out underneath.
- Join is a dialog that lists the games running right now, each with a Join button.
- The menu drawer holds only the site's pages: Dashboard, Announcements, All stories, Ranks & unlocks.
- The library can show just games or just solo writes.
- Cards no longer sit blank below the fold after a page loads, and scrolling no longer blinks.

## In the game

- If your connection drops for a moment, you keep your seat. The page says "Reconnecting…" and picks up where you were. No more looking kicked until a refresh.
- A stolen turn costs only the writer it was stolen from. Once the thief has written, the circle carries on with whoever was next.
- Host settings open in a dialog from the gear tab on the left edge. Pause and End game stay in the session bar.
- The host's Share menu can post to the Byler Offscreen Discord, copy the game link, or invite a friend.
- The chat stays beside the story at the reveal.
- Gimmick panels no longer wobble when their readout changes, and every one of them says "Put away" the same way.

## Solo editor

- Comment mode shows it is on: a strip at the top of the editor with Done, a speech-bubble cursor, and a ring around the page. Beta readers see "Reading to comment".
- Deleting or resolving a comment takes its underline out of the HTML view too.
- Clicking an underlined passage opens the comments drawer if it was closed.

## Small things

- The announcement card's frame no longer clips on the left.
- The host tab wears a gear and says "Host settings".
- Icons load from the site itself now, so pages open a touch faster.
