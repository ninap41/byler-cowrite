# Beta comments no longer interrupt the author

## Write while your beta reader reads

- A beta reader can comment, reply, edit, resolve and delete while you are typing, and your story keeps saving. You will no longer be told the story "was changed in another tab" because somebody left you a note.
- A new comment underlines its words in your editor the moment it arrives, even if you have unsaved typing. You no longer need to save or reload to see where it points, and you never have to give up your own words to read a comment.
- Logging a sprint, inviting a reader or changing who can see a story no longer pauses autosave either.

## Your existing comments

- Every comment, reply and suggestion already on your stories has been carried over. Nothing to do on your side.

## Under the hood

Comments are now kept separately from the story text, so the two can be written at the same time without getting in each other's way.
