# Public stories & Comic Sans

Share you stories with people not on Byler Cowrite!

## Public stories

- A public solo write now opens for anyone with the link, signed in or not!
- Readers see the story in the theme you choose: Share menu, Reader theme and can still switch.
- Readers get a clean page: no toolbar, no comments, chapter arrows and a chapter picker at the top and bottom, and can enlarge the text.

## Game chat

- Paste a link in the chat and it's clickable, with a little preview card (title and picture) under the message.

## Solo editor

- Comic Sans joins the typeface and theme font menus.
- On a phone, the Appearance menu opens properly.
