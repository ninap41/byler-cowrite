# Public stories, safer saving, and comment threads

Everything since the last release, in brief.

## Public stories

- A public solo write now opens for anyone with the link, signed in or not.
- Readers see the story in the theme you choose: Share menu, Reader theme. They can still switch.
- Readers get a clean page: no toolbar, no comments, chapter arrows and a chapter picker at the top and bottom.
- Readers can enlarge the text: Appearance, Text size.

## Solo editor

- Version history: earlier copies are kept as you work. Document menu, Version history. Download or restore any of them.
- "Saved" means stored. A failed save keeps trying and keeps your words. A story changed in another tab asks before overwriting.
- Deleting a chapter asks first.
- Comic Sans joins the typeface and theme font menus.
- Long chapters scroll smoothly again.
- On a phone, the Appearance menu opens properly.

## Comments

- Reply to a reply, and react with emoji.
- Reply, Edit, Add reaction and Delete live under the ⋯ on each message.
- An amber ! marks a comment whose words have changed.
- A reader's note is never lost to the author's save.

## Dashboard

- The writers directory filters by friends, word count and rank.
- A ? beside your streak explains how streaks work.
