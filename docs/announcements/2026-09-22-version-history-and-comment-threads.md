# Version history, safer saving, and comment threads

## Version history

- Solo writes now keep earlier copies of themselves as you work: about one every ten minutes while you write, one a day after that, and always the one from just before a lot of words or a chapter went missing.
- Document menu, Version history. Each copy says when it is from and how long it is. Download any of them, or restore one. A restore can be undone.

## Saving

- "Saved" means the words are stored. If something goes wrong, the page stays on "Unsaved", keeps trying, and keeps a copy in your browser.
- Anything you type while a save is in progress is kept.
- If the story was changed in another tab or on another device, saving pauses and asks you what to do. Nothing is overwritten without you choosing it.
- The Save button is greyed out when there is nothing to save and reads "Saving…" while a save is on its way.
- Deleting a chapter asks first and says how many words and open comments go with it.

## Comments

- Reply to a reply. Answers sit indented under the message they answer.
- React to a comment or a reply with an emoji, the same way as in a game's chat.
- Under each message: the reactions, then a ⋯ that holds Reply, Edit, Add reaction and Delete.
- Hover a commenter's name to see whether they are the author or a beta reader.
- A comment on words that have since changed shows a small amber ! you can hover for the explanation.
- A reader's note left while the author is saving keeps its place. A note on words the author just changed is handed back to the reader to send again. A half-written note survives the author's save.
