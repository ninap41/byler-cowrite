# Chapters, a fairer scoreboard, and a handful of fixes

Here is what changed today.

## Solo writes have chapters

- Your solo write is now a list of chapters. Add one, rename it, reorder with the arrows, or delete it (two clicks, so nothing goes by accident).
- The chapter panel sits on the left of the editor. Close it with the panel's own X, and the head chip reads "View chapters" while it is closed so you always know the way back.
- One chapter is open at a time. Prev and Next sit under the editor, and a link with `?ch=` opens a story on that chapter.
- Comments belong to the chapter they were made in. The comments rail shows the open chapter's notes and tells you how many there are in all.
- Export offers "This chapter" or "Whole work" as a styled .html file.
- Every existing story became a one chapter story on its own. Nothing moved, nothing was lost, and beta readers can comment on it right away.

## Beta readers

- When you invite a beta reader, anyone who is not a friend yet now has an Add friend button right on their row. Send the request from the picker and the row reads "requested".

## Games

- The scoreboard at the reveal now counts every writer who put words into the story, including earlier rounds of a continued game and writers whose seat has since expired. Nobody's words fall off the board.

## Phones

- The chapter list and the comments sheet now stand on a solid background, so the story no longer shows through them.

## Small things

- Chat and comment icons are plain again instead of wearing the theme gradient.
- The Insert image button in the toolbar shows the real picture emoji.
- The AO3 skin previewer moved to its own site and is linked from the tour bar, the menu and the dashboard.
