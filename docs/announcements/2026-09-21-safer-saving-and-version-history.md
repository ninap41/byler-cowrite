# Safer saving, and a way back: version history

One of you lost a few thousand words from a solo write this week. We got most of them back, and then went through everything that happens between your keyboard and the database to find every way that could happen. This update closes all of them.

## Version history

- Your solo writes now keep earlier copies of themselves as you work: about one every ten minutes while you write, one a day after that, and always the copy from just before a lot of words or a whole chapter went missing.
- Open the Document menu and choose Version history to see them. Each one says when it is from and how it differs from what is on the page now. You can download any of them, or restore one.
- Restoring keeps the copy you replaced, so you can change your mind.
- Only you can see your story's history. Beta readers can't.

## Saving you can trust

- "Saved" now means saved. The page only says it once your words are safely stored, and if something goes wrong on our side it stays on "Unsaved", keeps trying, and keeps a copy in your browser.
- If a save gets stuck on a bad connection, the editor gives up on it and tries again instead of quietly stopping.
- Anything you type, and any chapter you add, while a save is in progress is kept.
- Your browser's backup copy is written much more often: every thirty seconds while you have unsaved words, whenever a save fails, and when you switch away from the tab or lock your phone. Each story now has its own, so working on a second story never replaces the first one's.
- If the story was changed somewhere else, such as another tab or another device, saving is paused and you are asked what to do. Nothing gets overwritten without you choosing it, and what you typed is kept either way.

## Smaller things

- Deleting a chapter now asks first, and tells you how many words would go. A double-click can't do it any more.
- A "less than" sign typed in your prose stays what you typed.

## One habit worth keeping

Version history is there so an accident is two clicks to undo. For anything you would hate to lose, Document, Download, Whole work gives you your own copy too.
