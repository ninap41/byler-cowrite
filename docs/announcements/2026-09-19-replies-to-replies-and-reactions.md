# Replies to replies, and reactions on comments

Comment threads in solo writes got a tidier layout and two new things to do in them.

## What's new

- Reply to a reply. Every message in a thread has its own Reply, and the answer sits indented under what it answers, so a side conversation stays together.
- React to a comment or a reply with an emoji, the same way you do in a game's chat. Tap a reaction again to take it back, and hover one to see who left it.
- Reply, Edit and Delete now sit in a row under each message instead of behind a menu.
- The reply box opens right under the message you're answering and says who you're replying to.
- A long thread shows its first few replies with a "Show more" underneath, so one busy note doesn't bury the rest.

## What hasn't changed

- You can only edit your own words. The author can delete any comment or reply on their story.
- The author and invited beta readers can reply and react. Someone reading a public story can see the conversation but not join it.
- Resolve, Reject, Reopen, and accepting a suggested rewrite all work as before.
