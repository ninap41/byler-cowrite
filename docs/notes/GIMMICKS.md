# Gimmicks

Detailed notes, moved out of CLAUDE.md. A `public/js/x.js` path means the source `client/x.ts`; a page's "inline script" is `client/pages/<page>.ts`.

- **Gimmicks are rank rewards you PLAY** — toys used INSIDE a live session to distract the table, and the distraction is the point: **the dice tumble over the live game UI, the writer's editor included, and everyone in the session sees every die that's out.** The registry of what a gimmick is, plus its rules, is `lib/gimmicks.js` (pure: `GIMMICKS`, `rollOutcome`, `describeRoll`); and **each gimmick belongs to a THEME** (the `theme` field on its registry entry): `GIMMICK_UNLOCKS` in lib/achievements.js is DERIVED as `themeUnlocks[GIMMICKS[id].theme]` — unlock the theme, unlock its gimmick; retier the theme in achievements.json and the gimmick moves with it; a gimmick on a free/unknown theme is free. There is no separate gimmick map in achievements.json (its `_gimmickReadme` says so), labels come from the registry names, and the gate then works exactly like themes (`tierForGimmick`/`canUseGimmick`/`unlockedGimmicks`/`gimmickLocks` in lib/achievements.js; `GET /api/gimmicks` = `{catalogue, locks, unlocked, admin}`; `publicUser.gimmicks` and the `badge-earned` toast's `gimmicks` re-gate without a fetch; admins have every gimmick). v1 is the **Hellfire d20** (`d20`, unlocked with the Hellfire Club theme at `sorcerer` — Hellfire and Cerebro swapped tiers for it): a die in your own colour you drag anywhere on the screen, flick to throw, click to roll. **Only in non-friendly games** (`s.friendly === false`), only from a seat, and **a tablemate's rank hands the whole table the toy**: `game-state` carries `tableGimmicks` (ids ANY seated account has unlocked, admins included, `tableHasGimmick()`), and the 🎲 menu wears 🔓 · Play on those rows — a live row (`data-act="play"`, tip "A tablemate has this unlocked, so it's yours at this table"), so a lobby seating one admin lights every gimmick for everyone; 🎲 · Play for your own unlocks; 🔒 (disabled, tier named) when nobody has it. The server's `tableHasGimmick()` is the only gate on every relayed effect, so the menu and the server agree. Two events: **`gimmick-die {on, x, y}`** — the owner reports their die is out and where it sits as FRACTIONS of their own screen (throttled to one per 60ms client-side; `on:false` puts it away); the server keeps `s.dice` (userId → {name,color,x,y}, in memory only, never snapshotted), relays `gimmick-die {userId,name,color,x,y,on}` to the whole room (spectators too), sends the current list as `gimmick-dice` to anyone who seats or spectates, and drops a die (`dropDie`) when its owner disconnects, is removed, or the host makes the game friendly. And **`gimmick-roll {id, steal}`** — the server rolls (`randomInt`; `COWRITE_DICE_FIXED="20,1"` fixes the first values for tests, `COWRITE_ROLL_COOLDOWN_MS` shrinks the 1.8s per-user cooldown), calls every landing in writers chat via `announce()` ("rolled a 13 🎲" / "rolled a natural 1 🎲 — fumble." / "rolled a NATURAL 20 🎲 and stole the turn from Mike!"), emits `gimmick-roll {userId,name,color,value,kind,stole}` to the room so every client tumbles THAT die to the same face, and on a **natural 20** mid-writing (not paused, not already their turn, `steal !== false`) sets **`s.thiefId`** to the roller and `startTurn()`s — the stolen turn is held ON TOP of the order (`currentId(s)` returns the thief while set; `currentIdx` stays on the VICTIM's seat), so when the thief's turn ends `advance()` clears it and the circle resumes with whoever was after the victim: a steal costs its victim one turn and nobody else their place; a steal on a stolen turn just swaps the holder; `seatSocket` carries it across a reconnect, `removeWriter`/`startTurn` drop it when the thief leaves (the turn falls back to the victim's seat), every turn-order reset nulls it, and it is never snapshotted — `turnCount` is untouched, and **the interrupted writer's unsent line rides into the thief's editor**: `carryStolen()` (game.js, run after `startTurn` on both the d20 and Galaga steal paths) takes `lastTypingRaw`, appends `<i>[- <b>thief</b> stole from <b>victim</b> ]</i>` (names escaped, then `sanitizeRich`), sets it as the new `lastTyping`, emits `steal-carry {html}` to the thief (game.html fills `#writerEditor` and moves the caret to the end) and `live-typing` to the rest, so the timeout commits it as the thief's line. `steal: false` is the roller's **opt-out** (the HUD's "Steal the turn on a natural 20" box, remembered in localStorage `cowriteDiceSteal`): the crit is still called ("— and let the writer keep the turn."). **Sound: the tumble and the natural-20 line** — every throw plays `sounds/dierolling.mp3` (`dierolling` in `SOUND_NAMES`, category `gimmick`; game.html passes `onRoll: () => playSound("dierolling")` to `mountGimmickDice`, fired for my ack and for a tablemate's `gimmick-roll`), and `announce(…, {chime: true})` stamps `chime` on the natural-20 system message alone — `shouldChimeChat()` rings for a `sys` message iff `chime === true` (every other system line stays silent). Both play under the account's **`gimmick` sound preference** (`sounds.gimmick`, the fourth box in `/settings` — labelled just "Gimmick sounds" — beside chat/story/clock; `play(name, category)` takes the category override, so the chat ping rings under the gimmick pref and a muted chat still hears a natural 20). **v2 adds the Palace Arcade Galaga** (`galaga`, unlocked with the Palace Arcade theme at `explorer`): a playable mini-Galaga fought full-screen OVER the live game, the dice's way (`components/galaga-game.js` — the arcade theme's pixel sprites as `.gg-*` CSS; layer at z 60, pointer-events none, only the HUD glass takes the pointer; ship steered from the document: mouse/←→ move, Space or the HUD's Fire button shoots, keys ignored while focus is in an editor/input; 45s run, bobbing fleet + divers, POINTS 100 bob / 300 dive). **Every battle is shared**: each player's ship wears their own PALETTE colour (`shipShadow(color)` re-inks the sprite) and streams ship/fleet/shots as fractions of their own screen (**`gimmick-ship {on,x,score,shots,bees}`**, throttled 80ms; server clamps — max 10 bees / 4 shots — keeps `s.ships`, relays to the room incl. spectators, sends `gimmick-ships` to late joiners, and drops a battle on disconnect/removal/friendly, like `dropDie`), so several players blast their own fleets side by side and the whole table watches. Every ship — mine included — carries a `.gg-ship-tag` name·score under it, and while ANY battle is live `html.gg-live` fades the arcade THEME's own ambient fleet/ship/shot away (base.css) so the played Galaga is the only one on screen. The relayed score is display-only; only the FINAL score counts, going up as **`gimmick-galaga {score, steal}`**: the server clamps it (`galagaOutcome`, `GALAGA_TARGET = 3000`), announces via `describeGalaga` (chiming like a nat 20 on a high score), and a score OVER 3000 mid-writing steals the turn under exactly the d20's conditions and opt-out (same `cowriteDiceSteal` key). **v3 adds the Starcourt Milkshake** (`milkshake`, unlocked with the Starcourt theme at `practice`; `components/milkshake-spill.js`, adapted from the repo-root `jazz-milkshake.html` reference with GSAP swapped for a small rAF tween): a paper cup TINTED in the owner's colour (`cupSvg(color, key)` — SVG ids suffixed per cup; `shade()` darkens the gradient) you drag over the game and tip — click for a full pour, whip sideways to slosh — and the spill rains onto two canvases, pooling along the bottom of the screen over the chat dock — the merging-puddle model now lives in `components/puddle-ground.js` (`createGround`), shared with the SuperSoaker; puddles carry an `owner` and merge only per owner, so **Wipe up mops only YOUR mess**. Shared the same way: the owner streams the CUP (**`gimmick-cup {on,x,y,rot,level}`**, throttled; server clamps, keeps `s.cups`, relays, `gimmick-cups` for late joiners, `dropCup` on the same paths) and every viewer simulates the drops locally from that stream — no drop ever crosses the wire; **`gimmick-pour`** is the chat call ("tipped a milkshake over the game 🥤", per-user cooldown in `s.gimmickPours`, no chime, NO steal — pure distraction), and the pour animation only runs on an ok ack. HUD has Refill (local), Wipe up (clears this viewer's puddles) and Put away; the mess clears when the last cup leaves. **v4 adds the Rink-O-Mania Disco Ball** (`disco`, unlocked with the Rink-O-Mania theme at `puppymike`; `components/disco-ball.js`): a silver mirror ball (`ballHtml(key)` — a real CSS-3D sphere of mirror tiles turning inside a perspective stage, with core/shade/spec lighting overlays, halo and per-tile glints, adapted from the repo-root `disco-ball.html` reference and deterministic per key; not tinted, whose it is lives in the name tag) hung by a CSS chain to the top edge, dragged anywhere over the game; click to SPIN and for one show (`SPIN_MS` = 8s, also the per-user cooldown in `s.gimmickSpins`) the reference's light show runs around the ball — 8 spots per show, capped 16 across overlapping shows (a repeat spin replaces its owner's set, the cap trims the oldest), each a small blurred radial-gradient dot in `mix-blend-mode: screen` ORBITING the owner's ball on tilted elliptical sweeps (swelling/brightening on the near side, flickering; the orbit centre is re-read every frame so the lights follow a dragged ball), under a `.db-beams` fan of blurred beam cones turning in step, all driven by one shared rAF loop seeded from the owner's id, tinted the owner's colour first then the table's (`spotColors`; game.html feeds `getTableColors` from `game-state` writers). Shared the same way: the owner streams the BALL (**`gimmick-ball {on,x,y}`**, throttled; server clamps, keeps `s.balls`, relays, `gimmick-balls` for late joiners, `dropBall` on the same paths) and **`gimmick-spin`** is the ack'd chat call ("turned on the disco ball 🪩", no chime, NO steal — pure distraction) relayed with `duration` so every viewer — the spinner included — runs the show locally from that one event; no light spot ever crosses the wire, and the spinner's own show starts from the relay, so the lights and the chat call can never disagree. HUD has Spin and Put away; no sound. The 🎲 menu hands non-dice gimmicks to their own mount via `mountGimmickDice`'s `launchers: {id: fn}` opt — **a new gimmick must be LINKED in game.html** (a `launchers` entry plus a `gimmicksOff()` call on the friendly switch); the menu lists it from the catalogue automatically, so an unlinked entry would show and do nothing. `test/gimmicks.test.mjs` pins every registry id into both places. **Gimmicks switched off fade, and say so**: every mount exposes `gimmicksOff()` — when `game-state` flips a session to friendly, game.html calls all four; whatever is out (my die/ship/cup, everyone's remotes, the milkshake canvases) FADES away via `window.gsap` (instant when it isn't loaded — jsdom, blocked CDN) instead of blinking off, relayed `on:false` drops fade the same way, and if anything was out a `.gimmick-off-toast` ("💛 This is a friendly game now — gimmicks are off.") fades in over the foot bar. **Later gimmicks**: **Will's Art Room** (`artroom`, `components/art-room.js`) gives every painter their OWN offscreen layer — strokes and `destination-out` erases composite only on the owner's layer, `blit()` stacks layers newest-painter-on-top, a swatch may be any `#rrggbb` (`hexOr`; black and white included — never `safeColor`, which would fall back to red) and the colour picker follows the chosen swatch; the brush never paints over the chat dock (`overChat()` — a press there starts nothing, a captured stroke lifts at its edge). **SuperSoaker** (`supersoaker`, `components/super-soaker.js`) lands its drops on the shared **`components/puddle-ground.js`** (`createGround` — the milkshake's merging-puddle model with an `owner` per puddle, merges only per owner, `wipe(owner)`), pooling above the foot bar exactly like the milkshake; `button.sk-gunbtn:hover` re-states the centring translate so the global button lift can't throw the gun off the cursor. **Vecna's Curse** (`curse`, `components/vecna-curse.js`) holds `CURSE_MS` = 60s and the victim sings out by typing `CURSE_LIFT_WORDS` = 32 words (a word counts on its first letter, space/Enter ends it); the veil reads `WRITE. N/32 words · Ss` and counts down. **Every HUD glass is one mostly-solid panel with a width floor** (the "Gimmick HUD glass" rule just before the button rule in base.css: all seven HUDs `min-width: min(300px, 100vw − 32px)` and `--panel` at 92%, so a readout that changes never resizes the panel and the game under it is dimmed rather than fogged; `test/client-gimmicks.test.mjs` pins it). **Every HUD's action buttons share one compact size** (the "Gimmick HUD buttons" rule, LAST in base.css so it outranks every per-HUD rule: 30px tall, 0.78rem, `padding: 0 11px`, `flex: none` — Fire/Refill/Spin/Wipe/Put away on all seven glasses — and **the exit button reads "↩ Put away" on every HUD**, never "Put the cup away"/"Walk away"/"Leave the clock alone"; the art room's swatches and brush sizes and the curse's target list are pickers and keep their own; `test/client-gimmicks.test.mjs` pins the selectors and that nothing sizes a button after it). **Every HUD glass carries − and ×** (`hudCtlHtml()` in `components/gimmick-dock.js`: − folds the panel to its edge tab via the dock, the toy stays out; × is the component's exit) — and the host ⚙️ tab / `.gk-tab`s opt out of the global button hover lift. **Chat is in the page under the gimmick layers (z 60)**, so a spill can cover it — the distraction is the point; the art room still refuses to paint over `#chatCard` (`overChat()`). `test/client-galaga.test.mjs` / `test/client-milkshake.test.mjs` / `test/client-disco.test.mjs` pin the client halves; the server flows live in `test/gimmicks.test.mjs`. Client: `components/d20-die.js` is the reference `d20-roller-red.html` made a component — a real icosahedron, quaternion tumble, per-frame shading — that **rolls to a GIVEN value** and is tinted from the writer's PALETTE colour (`paletteRamps`); `components/gimmick-dice.js` owns the foot bar's 🎲 menu (`menuHtml`: Play / 🔓 a tablemate has it (disabled) / 🔒 Unlocks at… (disabled) / "friendly game — gimmicks are off") and the layer `#gimmickLayer` (**z 60**, under the foot bar and turn toast, never inside `.bg-layers`; NO theme peek — the game stays fully usable underneath). The layer takes no pointer events: only MY die (`#gdDie`, a button) and the small HUD glass (readout, steal box, "Put away") do; everyone else's dice (`#gdOthers .gd-die.remote`, name-tagged) are inert. Dice are positioned by `left/top` (the global button hover sets `transform`) and my die is dragged with pointer events: a release within 6px = roll, a flick over 650px/s glides it (`throwTo`) and rolls, anything else just moves it. The layer shows whenever ANY die is out (mine or theirs) and hides when none is; hidden tabs snap rolls and `settleNow()` on return. `test/gimmicks.test.mjs` (server) and `test/client-gimmicks.test.mjs` (jsdom) pin all of it.
