# Prompt modes and the guided generator

Detailed notes, moved out of CLAUDE.md. A `public/js/x.js` path means the source `client/x.ts`; a page's "inline script" is `client/pages/<page>.ts`.

- **Prompt modes** (`lib/prompt-gen.js`, pure — no I/O, no DOM; `docs/PROMPT_GENERATION.md` is the design doc): the vote ballot is dealt in one of two modes held on the session as `promptMode`/`promptControls`. **Simple** (default) deals untouched curated strings from `content/prompts.json`'s `prompts` array — never rewritten, never assembled. **Intermediate** ("Advanced" in the UI) assembles a prompt from **AXES** in `content/prompts.json`'s `intermediate` pools — `seasons` (pre-canon, S1–S5, `post-canon` labelled "Future fic (adults)"), `canon` (compliant / divergent / AU), `places`, `situations`, `relationships`, `tones` — plus **ONE weighted trope** from the `tropes` bank (grouped by `tropeGroups`: proximity, practice, shape, miscommunication, hurt-comfort, domestic, milestones, structure, setting-au, angst — nothing in it duplicates an axis: no place-shaped tropes, no slow burn/hurt-comfort/reunion/secret relationship) and, past the age gate, the **`explicit` layer** (levels none/suggestive/explicit; setups, dynamics, acts, weighted `kinks`, registers — tags written as short strings so they weight and combine the way AO3 tags do; hand-curated, never scraped). Rendered as **one bulleted `Category: choice` clause per LINE** (`bulletList()`, `CATEGORIES`): Season, Canon, Place, Relationship, Situation, one Trope line each, Tone, then `Rating: suggestive` — or `Rating: explicit` followed by ONE `Kinks:` line — setup · dynamic · acts · kinks · register, joined by `TAG_SEP`. It stays PLAIN TEXT on the wire and in snapshots; **`promptHtml()` in `public/js/util.js`** is how a page shows it — a `.prompt-grid` of category | choice rows (two column pairs from 720px), each category name wrapped in `.pc.pc-<category>` with a `title` tooltip from `PROMPT_CAT_TIPS` so it wears one consistent colour (base.css, end of file) on the game banners, the ballot `.option`s and the archive's `#archPrompt`; a prompt whose lines don't all open with a known category (curated, hand-written) comes back plain-escaped, so nothing user-typed becomes markup. The export's `h3.prompt` keeps the plain bulleted text. Anywhere a prompt stands in for a TITLE it collapses first — `oneLinePrompt()` in `public/js/util.js` client-side, `unbullet()` in the lib. Selection is seeded (`createSeededRandom` — same seed + same options rebuild the same prose) and weighted, recently-used ids damped rather than banned. **The season comes first and fixes the age** (`ageGroup` minor|adult — every on-screen season is minors, only post-canon is adult); **the rating is None or Explicit** (`suggestive` is DEPRECATED: `EXPLICIT_LEVELS = [none, explicit]`, off the wire it reads as none, an old pack's level row is filtered out of `/api/prompt-options` and `levelsFor`, no Rating: suggestive line exists) and **Explicit is only for S4, S5 and the future fic**: `allowsExplicit(season)` = adult OR tag `explicit-ok` (S4, S5 carry it), and pre-canon–S3 carry `incompatibleTags: ["explicit"]` so the SAME shared rule greys them in the Season menu once Explicit is chosen (and `levelsFor` takes Explicit off the menu once an early season is chosen — `fire()` narrows levels before judging menus so the two gates never fight). **The gate runs BEFORE any tag weighting** (`gateExplicit()`): an early season forces `explicit` to `none` and the kink layer is never drawn; explicit with the season on Random narrows the season draw to the explicit-capable pool; a chosen early season is always honoured (the level drops, the season doesn't); fluff or a kids' world with explicit asked also drops to none. `adultOnly` now means explicit-only content (the level, a world's explicit rooms): it reaches any explicit-capable season, S4/S5 included. **Canon comes second**: an AU always draws one world from `setting-au` (`compatibleCanon: ["au"]`), which never occupies the trope slot; **every world has its own rooms**: `intermediate.auPlaces` (~2,100 entries, ~60 per world, hand-approved) are places keyed by the world's `au-<id>` tag (`requiresTags`), the explicit ones ALSO requiring `explicit` (+ `adultOnly`), and under a world the Place line deals from that pool instead of the generic `places` (which are `compatibleCanon: [canon-compliant, canon-divergent]`, so a canon ballot never gets an AU room and an AU never gets "road trip"). Places read "a/an …" except superlatives, one-offs and proper names ("the last night before the siege", "the Creel house mid-investigation"). The high-school world is `incompatibleTags: ["explicit"]` — kids. `/api/prompt-options` ships `auPlaces` as id/label/rules rows (each `requires` its world's `au-<id>` tag) and **the Place menu follows the world** (`poolFor`/`worldInPlay` in prompt-modes.js: a chosen world with Canon on AU or Random lists that world's rooms, Random first; another world its own; canon the generic pool — a pick from the other pool falls back to Random), and the generator honours a pinned room from the pool in play (`pinnedRoom`) and deals around any other pin and on `/admin` the worlds are their own **AU worlds** section (`auWorldsHtml`/`auWorldHtml`/`readAuWorlds` in admin-view.js): one block per world — label · weight · rules JSON, then two textareas of places (everyday / explicit, a place per line) — plus an add-world row; the Tropes table hides `setting-au` rows, and `readPromptEditor` merges the section back as `setting-au` tropes (the `au-<id>` tag added for you, original bank order kept) and regenerates `auPlaces` from the textareas, so a removed world takes its rooms with it; fix-it is `canon-divergent` only. **Cleradin** (`cleradin` in `setting-au`, tags `fantasy`+`cleradin`: sorcerer Will, paladin Mike) has its own `cleradin` trope group (`requiresTags: ["cleradin"]`, weight 5 — the healing runs out, the oath forbids it, the tower before the siege…) and **nothing modern reaches a fantasy world**: phones/cars/mixtapes/prom/etc. tropes and car/shower/phone/filming explicit tags carry `incompatibleTags: ["fantasy"]`, which the kingdom AU shares. **Crack has its own trope group** (`crack`, `requiresTags: ["crack"]` — the Crack tone carries the tag — weight 8 so a crack game is mostly crack; the Hawkins-specific ones exclude `fantasy`) and the explicit layer has an optional `twists` pool (a ridiculous Hawkins twist appended to the Kinks line one time in five, `twistId` in selections; never in a fantasy world). **Fluff is never explicit** (tone tag `no-explicit` + `incompatibleTags: ["explicit"]`; the generator adds `explicit` to the active tags before the tone draw, and a host-chosen soft tone with explicit asked steps the rating down to none). **Explicit marks on the trope bank** (curated by hand, group by group): a trope that can't sit on an explicit ballot carries `incompatibleTags: ["explicit"]` — the soft domestic and milestone beats (cuddling, hand holding, first kiss, proposal…), the tender hurt/comfort and Cleradin ones, found family, second chance, the structural conceits, a few crack and angst levers — and everything else is neutral; `requiresTags: ["explicit"]` is the mark for an explicit-only trope, unused so far. **Relationships carry `together`/`not-together`/`exes` tags** and tropes + explicit setups say which they assume (`requiresTags`/`incompatibleTags` — getting-together tropes never land on a couple, domestic bliss/proposal/make-up sex need one). **Compatibility is a generation rule, not a UI one**: `isCompatible()` enforces `compatibleAgeGroups`/`compatibleCanon`/`incompatibleTags`/`requiresTags` and, crucially, `adultOnly` never reaches a minor season — so marriage-of-convenience, roommates, exes and the like are `compatibleAgeGroups: ["adult"]`. `fillOptions(s)` fills `s.options` plus a parallel `s.optionMeta` (seed + `selections` {seasonId, canonId, worldId?, placeId?, relationshipId, situationId, toneId, tropeIds[1], explicitLevel, explicit?} + `labels`, `null` for curated or hand-written entries) and both ride in `game-state`, so the vote card chips each option (`optionChipsHtml`, `CHIP_ORDER`, each trope its own chip). Host-only `set-prompt-mode {mode, controls}` switches modes or retunes the knobs — **each mode keeps its own ballot** (`s.optionSets`, `stashOptions`/`restoreOptions` in game.js: a mode switch stashes the set on screen with its components AND votes and restores the other mode's set if it has one, so Simple ⇄ Advanced and back loses nothing; only a knob change in the same mode, or a reroll (`shuffle-options`/`reroll-option` → `fillOptions`, which drops that mode's stash and no other) deals afresh and clears votes; the same mode with the same knobs is a no-op; `test/prompt-modes.test.mjs` pins it); `start-game` accepts the same two fields. Controls are `seasonId/canonId/worldId/placeId/situationId/relationshipId/toneId` (ids or `random`; `worldId` is a `setting-au` trope id — the **AU world** menu, `onlyWhen: {canonId: "au"}` in `GUIDED_FIELDS`, shown only while Canon is Alternate universe and read as Random when hidden via `paintDependents()`; a chosen world with Canon on Random implies `au`, so picking Cleradin means Cleradin), and `explicitLevel` (defaults to `none`, NEVER random — nobody gets an explicit ballot they didn't ask for), plus **the explicit dropdowns** `setupId/dynamicId/actId/kinkId` (pins on the Kinks line; **registers are deprecated**: the pool may stay in a pack and is validated when present, but it is never dealt, never shipped and not a control: `GUIDED_FIELDS` entries with `onlyWhen: {explicitLevel: [suggestive, explicit]}` and `explicitOnly: true` — SHOWN once the level leaves None, shown only at Explicit, forgotten to Random when hidden; `/api/prompt-options` ships `setups/dynamics/acts/kinks/registers` as id/label/rules rows — never clause text — so `paintCompat` greys a modern setup under a fantasy world; in the generator a pinned setup/dynamic/register is taken as-is and a pinned act/kink LEADS its list with the rest dealt around it, all of it moot under the gate); and **`kinkCount`** (1..`MAX_KINKS` = 3, default 1, never random — a narrow `.pm-count` select `#…KinkN` beside the Kink menu, disabled with it; the generator deals EXACTLY that many kinks, a pinned one leading, acts stay 1–2; `cleanPromptControls` reads anything out of range as 1), and **the off switches** `situationOff/toneOff/setupOff/dynamicOff/actOff/kinkOff` (booleans, `OFF_KEYS`; a `.pm-off` checkbox beside the Situation and Tone labels and each explicit label — drawn from scratch (`appearance: none`, an `inline-grid` box with a `::before` mark placed by `place-content: center`) so the mark sits dead centre instead of drifting under the site's input padding, `off:` on the `GUIDED_FIELDS` entry — checked, the menu greys to Random and the generator never draws that part, so it never reaches the card: no line, no chip, no tags; the explicit boxes wake with their menus at Explicit; `cleanPromptControls` takes only a real `true`; `setState` paints them back without firing); everything off the wire goes through `cleanPromptMode`/`cleanPromptControls` first. `GET /api/prompt-options` ships ids+labels plus each row's RULES (`tags`/`requires`/`excludes`/`ageGroups`/`canon`/`adultOnly` — never clause text, never the trope bank; the explicit pools ship the same way for the dropdowns) so the client can grey out what the generator would refuse: `activeContext(menus, controls)` gathers the tags in play the way `isCompatible` would and `optionAllowed(row, ctx)` applies the same rules; `paintCompat()` judges each menu against the OTHER choices (so a pick never disables itself) and a pick that has become impossible falls back to Random. `levelsFor()` narrows the Explicit menu to what the chosen season admits and drops Explicit under a `no-explicit` tone (a level that falls out falls back to None). The vote-card mount also takes `onReroll` → a "🎲 Reroll all" button (`shuffle-options`) beside the knobs; the lobby mount has none. The client half is `public/js/components/prompt-modes.js`, which owns BOTH the markup (`promptModeHtml(prefix)`) and the wiring (`mountPromptModes(root, {prefix, onChange})`) so the two places that offer it can't drift: `game.html` mounts it in the lobby (`#lobbyPrompt`, host-only — the choice rides along in `start-game`) and on the vote card (`#votePrompt`, which emits `set-prompt-mode` on every change). Every id is prefixed because one page holds two mounts. Simple hides the guided knobs, and that needs BOTH halves: `paint()` toggles `.hidden` on `#…Controls`, and `base.css` re-declares the hidden state after the class's own `display:` (see **`.hidden` is only as strong as its source order** below). Advanced stays disabled until `/api/prompt-options` confirms the pools exist, and `setState()` paints an incoming broadcast WITHOUT firing `onChange` back at the server. `validateIntermediateData()` (run by `test/prompt-gen.test.mjs`) rejects duplicate/non-slug ids, empty clauses, a trope in an unnamed group, a library with no adult season, and an `explicit` level that isn't `adultOnly`.

- **A first meeting has no relationship**: the `first-meeting` situation carries the `first-meeting` tag and EVERY relationship is `incompatibleTags: ["first-meeting"]`; the generator draws the situation BEFORE the relationship and, on a first meeting, deals no Relationship line at all (no id, no chip — a host's relationship pick is ignored); the client disables the whole Relationship select ("A first meeting has no relationship yet") on top of the shared per-row greying. Likewise **a reunion never opens on a couple**: the `reunion` situation carries the `reunion` tag and new-couple / established / secret-relationship are `incompatibleTags: ["reunion"]`; the menu greys them and a pick that becomes impossible falls back to Random, and the generator deals AROUND a pinned relationship the situation rules out (`isCompatible` check before `resolve`) rather than honouring it. Tests in `test/prompt-gen.test.mjs`, `test/client-prompt-modes.test.mjs`.
