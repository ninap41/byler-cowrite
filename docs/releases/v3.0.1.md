# v3.0.1 — Repaired apostrophes, comment threads, and a calmer solo editor

Everything shipped since September 15. The headline is a fix: stories whose quotes and apostrophes had turned into long runs of "amp;" are repaired, and it cannot happen again.

## Fixes
- **Mangled quotes and apostrophes are repaired.** Some solo stories showed every " and ' as a long string of "amp;amp;amp;" that grew each time the story saved. Saving no longer does this, and a story that was affected is put right the moment it is opened. Nothing to do on your side, and no words were lost.
- **Word counts are right again.** A word with an apostrophe counts as one word, in the editor and on your shelf.
- **A chapter that is too long is refused, never cut.** The editor tells you to split it in two instead of quietly trimming its end.
- **A story too large to save in one piece says so** in plain words, instead of "Something went wrong".

## Comments in the solo editor
- **Beta comments no longer interrupt the author.** A beta reader can comment, reply, edit, resolve and delete while you are typing, and your story keeps saving. You will no longer be told the story "was changed in another tab" because somebody left you a note.
- **A new comment underlines its words the moment it arrives,** even if you have unsaved typing. No need to save or reload to see where it points.
- **Comments are threads.** Anyone who can comment can reply. Every voice wears an "author" or "beta" tag.
- **The author decides.** Resolve or Reject on a note, Accept or Reject on a suggested rewrite. A reader can resolve their own note.
- **Edit or delete your own words** from the ⋮ menu on a comment or a reply. The author can remove a reader's note but never rewrite it.
- **Closed threads fold to one line.** Click one to read it again, or reopen it from its menu.
- **Every comment already on your stories has been carried over.**
- Logging a sprint, inviting a reader or changing who can see a story no longer pauses autosave.
- The comments heading counts the notes in the open chapter only.

## Writing in the solo editor
- **Your story saves itself to the server** every 30 seconds once you pause typing, without moving your caret or your undo history.
- **Two tabs can't overwrite each other.** If the same story was saved from another tab, you are asked to reload or to save over it, and your words stay put until you choose.
- **Three menus instead of a crowded row:** Share (who can see the story, invite a beta reader), Document (comment mode, chapters, roll a prompt, sprint, downloads) and Appearance (line spacing, typeface, paper).
- **Find and replace.** Ctrl or ⌘ + F, with match case and whole word.
- **Share / Download menu:** copy the formatted story, or download it as a web page or a PDF.
- **Chapter arrows sit at the foot of the editor,** beside a shorter hint line.
- Save sits beside the Rich text / HTML switch, and the people reading along are shown beside the menus.
- The page runs edge to edge, with a comfortable margin around the prose at every screen size, phones included.

## Playing a game
- **Everyone hears the clock** through the last ten seconds of a turn, spectators too, for as long as the demogorgon dances.
- The Galaga screen says how to fire and steer.
- The SuperSoaker is a proper water pistol again.

## For admins
- An admin can delete any solo write. Its author gets a note in their inbox saying who removed it.

## Under the hood
Comments are now kept separately from the story text so the two can be written at the same time, the project's feature notes were split up by topic, and there is a written plan for making saving simpler and lighter.
