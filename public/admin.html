🛡️ Admin

Moderation tools. Everything here acts on other people's work: there's no undo.

Games in progress

Ending a game shows its writers the reveal; the story is kept and stays continuable.

Accounts

Sorted by how long they've been gone. Removing an account frees its username and signs it out everywhere; the lines it wrote stay in their stories. Admin accounts can't be removed.

Badges

The rank ladder and the collectible badges. Saving swaps the catalogue in at once: a new badge can be earned on the very next story line, a new rank on the next word count. Ids are permanent once saved, they live in every account's badge list.

Prompt library

Everything the ballot is dealt from: the Simple-mode scenarios, every Advanced axis, the trope bank and the explicit layer. Edits apply to the next ballot dealt; the whole library is validated before anything is saved.

Writers' reference

The word bank behind the "/" palette in solo writes, by category, one word or phrase per line. Empty a category to remove it. Each group saves on its own and applies the next time the palette opens.

Content backup

Everything above as files: the content pack and the writers' reference exactly as this site holds them right now, zipped in the repo's own layout (content/, writers-reference/) so it can be dropped back in.

Email (SMTP)

Password-reset links go out over SMTP. When a reset says "try again later", this check connects and logs in with the configured secrets and shows the real reason.
