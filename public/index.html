What is Byler Cowrite?

A real-time, round-robin writing game for Byler fanfiction. Everyone holds the pen.

  • ✍️ Round-robin writing — take turns adding one line each under a ticking countdown. The server owns the clock: when time's up, whatever you were typing is committed and play moves on.
  • 🗳️ Scenario votes — every game opens with a group vote on curated Byler prompts, and anyone can add their own scenario to the ballot.
  • ⌨️ Live typing — watch the current writer's line appear as they type it, with rich text (bold / italic / underline).
  • 💬 Docked chat — a collapsible chat panel with unread counts and message sounds, plus incoming/outgoing line sounds.
  • 👤 Accounts — sign up with email + username + password to play: your profile keeps your color, word count, badges, and every game you've written in.
  • 🏆 Badges — a word-count ladder from 🐶 Puppy Mike (5,000 words) to 💛 A Best "Friend" (100,000), plus secret badges earned by writing… the right thing. Announced in chat and worn next to your name.
  • 📚 Previous games — a private archive of every game you wrote in, with a Continue button to pick any story back up.
  • 🔑 Permanent codes — every game keeps its 4-letter code forever. Rejoin from any device just by logging in and entering the code.
  • 🚪 Host-gated joins — started or continued games require host approval for new (or returning) writers, with a 5-minute cooldown if the host says no.
  • 👑 Host controls — name the story, pause/resume, retune turn length and rounds mid-game, approve joins, end & reveal, and continue after the reveal.
  • 🌐 Dashboard — who's online, games in progress with Join buttons, and your recent stories.
  • 🟢 Presence everywhere — live online/offline dots beside every name: roster, chat, story lines, even old messages update in place.
  • 📤 Export — when the story's done, copy it as formatted rich text or download a clean, styled HTML file (bylines stripped, prose only).
  • 🎨 Nine themes — Neon Dusk, Aurora, Inkwell, The Wall, Snow Ball, Upside Down, Starcourt, Palace Arcade, and Cerebro, with GSAP-animated everything.
